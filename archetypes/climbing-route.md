Add approximate area coordinates to `area_latitude` and `area_longitude` when
you add a new location. These are area pins, not exact route coordinates.
Use `discipline: "Boulder"` with a V-scale grade, or `discipline: "Sport"`
with a Yosemite Decimal System grade such as `5.10a`. Use `setting: "Indoor"`
for gym climbs. The `personal_note` field is shown as a short reflection on
the route page; use this body for a longer trip report, beta, or training notes.
