First "crux": get left hand as high on the side pull then stand up into a big right.    Biggest crux: left heel in dish and left hand on a high side pull. Flag deep, pull right to terrible pocket, crimp the edge, lock off on right arm and reach up big left to the good lip on the edge.      Top: work left hand to the good hold just below/left of the final crimps, right hand to the right-more crimp, left to side pull besides sharp dagger, top out.
